## Context
A regional distribution business with 12 retail locations and regional warehouses was compiling monthly sales, inventory holding, and collections manually. Each branch submitted an independently formatted workbook at the end of each billing cycle.

## Problem
The central management team was spending nearly a full working week reconciling discrepancies between branch submissions and warehouse stock ledgers.
- Inconsistent row naming and product category classifications across branches.
- Broken Excel formulas from manual copy-pasting by branch managers.
- No audit trail for credit sales adjustments or returned inventory.
- Senior management had no visibility into branch performance until the 10th of the following month.

## What I did
- Standardized data input sheets with cell-level validation rules, drop-down lists, and locked calculation formulas.
- Built an automated data ingestion workflow using Power Query to consolidate 12 branch workbooks in seconds.
- Created a single executive summary pack featuring key metric cards: revenue, collections, aging receivables, and inventory turnover.
- Added automated error-flagging sheets that highlight unbalanced ledgers and duplicate entries before consolidation.
- Conducted training for branch accounts staff and provided a 4-page SOP document.

## Result
- Reduced monthly close turnaround time from 5 business days to under 4 hours.
- Eliminated formula breakdown errors and manual transcription mistakes completely.
- Provided leadership with standardized, reliable visibility into branch performance on Day 1 of every month.
