## Context
A multi-vendor infrastructure project office was coordinating simultaneous rollouts across regional sites. Changes in site conditions, equipment models, and delivery timelines were occurring daily across engineering, procurement, and field teams.

## Problem
Scope revisions, material substitutions, and engineering variations were being agreed upon informally through email threads, chat apps, and verbal discussions.
- Unrecorded variations led to invoicing disputes with subcontractors at milestone completion.
- Engineering and logistics teams were working against different versions of site specifications.
- Project managers lacked a consolidated view of pending change approvals and associated cost impacts.

## What I did
- Designed a centralized Change-Control Register (CCR) with unique tracking IDs, categorized priority tiers, and standardized status lifecycles.
- Established an impact assessment protocol calculating budget variance and schedule delay for every submitted change request.
- Developed a weekly variance digest for project directors highlighting pending, approved, and rejected variations.
- Documented a straightforward 1-page change management workflow with explicit sign-off thresholds.

## Result
- Achieved complete transparency across 150+ change events with zero unverified contractor invoices.
- Reduced dispute resolution cycles from weeks to single review sessions.
- Provided management with real-time foresight on cumulative budget impact before executing variations.

## What you can see
The architecture features clear ID taxonomy, status gates, automatic color-coded aging alerts, and an executive variance breakdown.
