## Context
A growing business services company had scaled rapidly from 5 to 25 team members. Operational workflows that previously worked via informal conversations began experiencing miscommunications, forgotten client deliverables, and delayed billing.

## Problem
Department handoffs between client intake, technical execution, and billing lacked standard operating procedures.
- Customer project statuses lived in individual staff members' notebooks or personal files.
- Deliverable sign-offs were frequently delayed, pushing invoicing cycles back by several weeks.
- Management spent entire weekly meetings verbally asking each team member for basic status updates.

## What I did
- Mapped end-to-end client service workflows and created clear stage gates with defined ownership for each transition.
- Built a lightweight, centralized master pipeline tracker with conditional formatting, status dropdowns, and automated due-date countdowns.
- Created step-by-step checklists and 1-page SOPs for new project onboarding, deliverable sign-off, and handover.
- Established a compact 1-page weekly management KPI summary displaying throughput, billable utilization, and bottleneck alerts.

## Result
- Eliminated overdue deliverable blindspots and improved on-time client handoffs by 40%.
- Shortened weekly management review meetings from 2 hours to a targeted 30-minute action session.
- Client invoicing turnaround shortened by 12 days, noticeably improving operating cash flow.

## What you can see
A streamlined pipeline tracker with built-in validation, status indicators, and an executive KPI summary that can be operated without proprietary software overhead.
